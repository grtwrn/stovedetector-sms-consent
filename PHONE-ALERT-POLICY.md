# StoveDetector phone alerts

Optional texts and automated calls about stove and oven alarms for devices in your household account.

To enroll, sign in, open Menu → Texts & calls, and request a verification text. Enter the code, then choose texts, automated calls, or both, and save your preferences. Verifying a number alone does not enable alarm texts or calls.

Texts and calls are optional and are not required to purchase or use StoveDetector.

## Phone alert terms

US mobile numbers are supported. Frequency varies with alarms. Message and data rates may apply. Each alarm can send one text and one automated call to each person who has enabled that channel. Calls start after 60 seconds if the alarm is still active and nobody has acknowledged it.

Reply STOP to stop texts and calls, or disable your preferences or remove your number in the app. After opting out by text, reply START to the same number, then enable your choices in the app to resume. START alone does not enable alarm texts or calls. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@stovedetector.com.

Once your number is verified, text STATUS for the latest reported device status. The app shows your daily status allowance. After that allowance, one reply points you to the app; further STATUS requests receive no reply until midnight UTC. Requests and replies may both incur message charges. A STOP opt-out also blocks requested status replies.

Delivery requires an internet connection from the device and a reachable phone. Messages and calls can be delayed or fail. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Sending limits may apply. Acknowledgment stops further calls for that incident and does not silence the kitchen buzzer.

## Phone alert privacy

StoveDetector stores your verified phone number, alert preferences, consent version and dates, and delivery records to provide the alerts you request. Phone numbers and alert content are sent to our delivery provider, Telnyx, so it can send the text or place the call.

Phone numbers and messaging consent are not sold or shared for third-party marketing. Your phone number is visible in your own account settings and is not shown to other household members.

Verification codes expire after 10 minutes and are stored as keyed hashes. Expired verification records are removed during regular cleanup. Delivery records and closed incidents are retained for up to 30 days before regular cleanup. Pending or uncertain deliveries may be retained longer for reconciliation. Removing your number stops future enrollment-based alerts. An opt-out record is retained to honor STOP.
